Your world, your data.

On this device

Tasks, notes, memos, lists, events, and progress are saved in browser local storage. They remain on that browser profile until you clear site data. This storage is readable by someone with access to your browser profile. No planner database is hosted by Pixel Quest Daybook.

The diary is stored as password-encrypted data using AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2-SHA256. Keys remain in memory while unlocked. The diary locks when you hide the tab or after five minutes of inactivity.

Local storage and manual backups

Tasks, notes, memos, lists, events, and progress are saved in this browser’s local storage. Pixel Quest Daybook has no account sign-in or hosted planner database. Browser data stays on this device until you clear site data.

Downloaded planner backups are encrypted in your browser using your backup password. Pixel Quest Daybook does not save or send that password. Restoring a backup replaces the current browser planner.

Reminders and third-party services

If you configure email reminders, your recipient email, task and event titles, due times, and a shared secret go to the Google Apps Script URL you provide. Diary and note content is not included. The script stores a schedule and sends mail through your account. Remove its time trigger and deployment to disable the service.

Google Fonts load from Google. Pixel Quest Daybook does not use Google account sign-in or request Google Drive access. Hosting providers handle ordinary page requests. Pixel Quest Daybook adds no analytics trackers.

Backups, deletion, and recovery

Downloaded backups contain an encrypted copy of the planner. To delete local data, use your browser’s site-data controls. To remove reminder schedules, remove the Apps Script project or its saved properties. Export a backup before clearing browser data.

There is no password recovery. Possession of the encrypted file and its password allows decryption using compatible software; access cannot be guaranteed exclusive to Pixel Quest Daybook.
