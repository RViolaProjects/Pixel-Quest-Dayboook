Pixel Quest Daybook

A little progress, every day.

A small world for everyday progress

I wanted a planner that felt like returning to a quiet little game world. Pixel Quest Daybook brings tasks, notes, lists, a calendar, focus sessions, and a locked diary into one pixel-inspired space.

Quests earn experience, focus sessions reward consistency, and encrypted backups help carry a personal world between devices.

Explore the project

The portfolio demo works in your browser. Your own entries stay on your device; they are not shared with other visitors or with the project creator. The planner works locally in this browser, and encrypted backup files let you move it between devices.

Try the planner ↗ · Backup & reminders · Privacy

Made to keep growing

The project uses plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with browser storage, Web Crypto, and an optional Apps Script reminder service. Theme colors and creator details are editable in the source files.

This is a browser-based portfolio project. Pixel Quest Daybook has no sign-in or server database. The diary and downloaded backups use passwords for encryption. Backups move only through files you download and restore manually.
